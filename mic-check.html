5b. Recognition while your voice is also being recorded

The game records the attempt for playback at the same time as it recognises it — two captures of one microphone. On some Windows machines the second capture is exactly what leaves the recogniser with silence, which looks like “it recorded fine but scored 0%”. Say water again. If section 4 heard you and this one does not, that is the fault.

6. Network, and on-device recognition

Chrome normally sends your audio to Google and waits for text back. If that route is blocked, recognition hangs and returns nothing. Chrome can also run it entirely on your own machine — this checks whether that is possible here.
Heads up: “Check on-device” can freeze the page for a few seconds while Chrome answers, and “Download language pack” may take a minute or two. Both are normal. Wait it out.
